## :bee: Description
An organization website that provides a centralized hub for streamlined
management. Students can register effortlessly, engage on a freedom wall, and
organization officers can post events and announcements easily.

## :link: WebHosting Link
Visit [OrgBee](http://orgbee.online/) now!


## :star: Features
- User/Student Registration: Students can sign up and create profiles. Once registered, they can access the community wall, participate in events, and view the announcements.
- Event Posting: Organization officers can post events with detailed information and requirements.
- Announcements: Important updates and announcements can be posted by organization officers, ensuring everyone stays informed.
- Freedom Wall: A community space for students to engage, share ideas, and discuss topics.
- Archive: Centralized storage for past events.

## :writing_hand: How it works
1. Register to the app
2. Verify your email
3. Wait for an officer to accept your membership
4. Explore the site !
